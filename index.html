Publications

13 selected papers
2026CVPR
VLAOpen-World

Percept-WAM: Perception-Enhanced World-Awareness-Action Model for Robust End-to-End Autonomous Driving ↗

Unifies 2D perspective-view and 3D bird's-eye-view perception as world tokens inside one VLM, then connects those representations directly to trajectory prediction.

Why it matters. Spatial grounding and action planning can share a foundation-model backbone without reducing perception to text-only reasoning.

2026CVPR
VLARobotics

AtomicVLA: Unlocking the Potential of Atomic Skill Learning in Robots ↗

Jointly generates plans, atomic skill abstractions, and low-level actions, using a skill-guided mixture of experts to compose specialized capabilities.

Why it matters. Modular skill routing offers a practical path from monolithic policies toward continual and long-horizon robot learning.

2026arXiv
VLAWorld Model

4D-WAM: 4D Consistent World Modeling for Autonomous Driving ↗

Uses geometric foundation models as training-time supervisors so predicted driving futures preserve 4D structure, with no additional inference cost.

Why it matters. Visually plausible futures are not enough; physically grounded supervision matters where decisions begin to form.

2026arXiv
VLAWorld Model

SUV: Future Scene Understanding as Video Generation for End-to-End Driving ↗

A shared video expert predicts future RGB, semantics, relative depth, and instance dynamics while an action expert reads their latent tokens to plan trajectories.

Why it matters. Generative future prediction can become a common interface between structured scene understanding and control.

2026arXiv
VLAEfficient Inference

WAM-Diff2: Hierarchical AR-to-Diffusion Distillation for Highly Efficient Autonomous Driving VLA ↗

Transfers an autoregressive driving generalist into a parallel discrete-diffusion model through hierarchical distillation and block-causal attention.

Why it matters. Generation-paradigm conversion can reduce VLA latency while retaining perception, reasoning, and planning capabilities.

2025ICCV
MultimodalGeneration

ILLUME: Illuminating Your LLMs to See, Draw, and Self-Enhance ↗

Builds a unified multimodal model around visual understanding and generation, then uses generated visual data to iteratively improve its own capabilities.

Why it matters. Understanding and generation are complementary learning signals rather than separate endpoints.

2025CVPR
MultimodalSpeech

EMOVA: Empowering Language Models to See, Hear and Speak with Vivid Emotions ↗

Aligns vision, text, and speech end to end with a semantic-acoustic disentangled tokenizer and lightweight controls for expressive speech styles.

Why it matters. Careful tokenization can align additional modalities without requiring scarce fully paired multimodal data.

2024ECCV
MultimodalReasoning

Reason2Drive: Towards Interpretable and Chain-Based Reasoning for Autonomous Driving ↗

Introduces a large video-text benchmark that decomposes driving decisions into perception, prediction, and reasoning chains, plus an evaluation metric for those chains.

Why it matters. Interpretable driving requires evaluating the decision process, not only the final answer or trajectory.

2025IJCV
MultimodalOpen-World

HiLM-D: Enhancing MLLMs with Multi-Scale High-Resolution Details for Autonomous Driving ↗

Combines low-resolution temporal reasoning with a high-resolution spatial stream and a lightweight adapter for fine-grained risk-object understanding.

Why it matters. Efficient multimodal systems should allocate visual resolution according to temporal and spatial information needs.

2025TPAMI
VLARobotics

NavCoT: Boosting LLM-Based Vision-and-Language Navigation via Learning Disentangled Reasoning ↗

Structures navigation reasoning into future imagination, visual observation matching, and action prediction, then learns the process with parameter-efficient tuning.

Why it matters. An embodied agent can use an LLM as both a lightweight predictive model and an interpretable decision module.

2022NeurIPS
Open-WorldMultimodal

DetCLIP: Dictionary-Enriched Visual-Concept Paralleled Pre-Training for Open-World Detection ↗

Reformulates heterogeneous detection data into parallel visual-concept learning and enriches category supervision with a concept dictionary.

Why it matters. Language knowledge can expand a detector beyond the closed label set while keeping pre-training efficient.

2023CVPR
Open-WorldMultimodal

DetCLIPv2: Scalable Open-Vocabulary Object Detection Pre-Training via Word-Region Alignment ↗

Learns direct word-region alignment from large-scale image-text pairs under unified detection, grounding, and caption supervision.

Why it matters. Fine-grained alignment makes web-scale image-text data useful for localization without expensive pseudo-label pipelines.

2024CVPR
Open-WorldMultimodal

DetCLIPv3: Towards Versatile Generative Open-Vocabulary Object Detection ↗

Adds object-level caption generation and hierarchical concept labels to open-vocabulary detection, bridging localization and dense description.

Why it matters. A detector becomes more versatile when recognition and object-centric generation reinforce each other.
